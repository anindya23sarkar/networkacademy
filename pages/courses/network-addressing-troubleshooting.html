COURSE OVERVIEW

What you'll explore

A foundation-level course focused on network addressing concepts and the first steps in diagnosing common connectivity issues.

  • Network addressing fundamentals
  • Basic connectivity checks
  • A structured approach to troubleshooting

Learning outcomes

By the end of this course, learners should be able to:

  • Explain how physical, data-link and network-layer functions support end-to-end connectivity.
  • Implement an IPv6 addressing scheme and describe Cisco routers and switches.
  • Troubleshoot basic network connectivity issues.

Who is this course for?

Learners who want to strengthen their IP addressing and basic troubleshooting foundations.
